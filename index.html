How Platform Works ⚡

1
Local Wallet Setup (Wallet)
No external extensions like MetaMask are required. Enter a password to generate a local wallet. Your private key is encrypted locally using AES in your browser.
2
Explore & Fund (Home / Explore)
Browse all active projects deployed on the smart contract. Support projects by contributing ETH directly into the secure contract vault using the Contribute action.
3
Create Your Own Launch (+)
Click the "+" button in the bottom menu to submit your project title, media, overall funding target, and milestone release percentages directly to the blockchain.
4
Milestone Escrow Mechanism
All raised funds are locked inside the smart contract escrow. Project creators cannot withdraw the full amount at once. Funds are unlocked gradually as each predefined milestone is completed.
5
Community Oversight & Challenge
If a creator fails to deliver promised milestones or misleads the community, backers can trigger a Challenge process to halt fund distribution.
