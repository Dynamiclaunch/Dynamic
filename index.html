How Platform Works

1
Local Wallet Setup (Wallet)
No external extensions required. Generate a local wallet encrypted with AES in your browser.
2
Explore & Fund (Home / Explore)
Browse active projects and contribute ETH directly into the smart contract vault.
3
Create Your Own Launch (+)
Submit your title, image, funding goal, and milestone allocations directly to the chain.
4
Milestone Escrow Mechanism
Funds are locked in smart contract escrow and released as milestones are completed.
